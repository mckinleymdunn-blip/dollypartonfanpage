Dolly Parton's Achievements

Dolly's Fun Facts

  • She moved to Nashville the day after she graduated from high school
  • She created the Dolly Parton Imagination Library to promote literacy
  • She has released over 50 studio albums
  • She has written or co-written over 3,000 songs
  • She has been a member of the Grand Ole Opry since 1966
