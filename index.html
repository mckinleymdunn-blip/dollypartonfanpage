General Information about Dolly.

Dolly Parton – Her life and career together make a rags-to-rhinestones story which began on January 19, 1946.

Born the fourth child of 12, Dolly grew up in Locust Ridge, nestled against the Great Smoky Mountains. Music runs deep in the Parton family and while many played important parts in Dolly’s success, she credits Uncle Bill Owens for helping her get started in the music business.
